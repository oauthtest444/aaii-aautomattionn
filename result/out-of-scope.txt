# Discovered but out of scope (not tested)

- sptpub-infra.com and *.sptpub-infra.com — related infrastructure domain, not
  authorized by data/targets.txt. Referenced by dragonclub.sptpub.com CNAME.
- CloudFront distribution domains (*.cloudfront.net) — third-party CDN
  infrastructure. Resolved via DNS to check for dangling CNAMEs, but no HTTP
  request was sent directly to a *.cloudfront.net host.
- S3 buckets not referenced by an in-scope *.sptpub.com host (e.g. hypothetical
  `spt77-dynamic-files`) — scope expansion. Not tested. Direct S3 endpoints were
  queried read-only only for `spt77-static`, `spt77-nondynamic-files`, and
  `bt-app-static-themes`, which are referenced by in-scope hosts.
- External operator domains (bettilt.com, blaze.com, bcgame.com, etc.) and any
  third-party payment/identity providers they use.
- console.byteplus.com — owner of creds/test-account.txt; unrelated to this
  target.
- Netlify/Cloudflare/S3 infrastructure itself; HTTP requests were limited to
  *.sptpub.com endpoints and the three referenced S3 bucket endpoints above.
