# Out-of-scope discoveries — account.ui.com run

Recorded per AGENT.md Section 2. These assets were observed only as part of
understanding the in-scope `account.ui.com` application. No testing was performed
against them beyond what is noted here.

- `https://evilaccount.ui.com` — wildcard DNS name observed while checking the
  CORS trust boundary; resolves to CloudFront. It was **not** fetched or tested as
  an application. It is not an authorized target.
- `https://uic.uidev.tools` — third-party origin explicitly trusted by the
  `sso.ui.com` CORS policy. One unauthenticated request returned HTTP 403 with
  CloudFront headers. It is outside the authorized scope and was not tested
  further.
- `https://*.ui.com` and `https://*.ubnt.com` — sibling subdomains trusted by the
  SSO/geo CORS policy. Not authorized and not enumerated or tested.

Related in-scope observation: `sso.ui.com` and `geo.svc.ui.com` reflect
`https://*.ui.com`, `https://*.ubnt.com`, apex `ui.com`, and
`https://uic.uidev.tools` in `Access-Control-Allow-Origin` with credentials.
Exploitation would require an attacker-controlled page on one of those origins,
which was not established. See `result/findings/observations.md` #7.
